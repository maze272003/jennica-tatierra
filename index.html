✍️ Original Story: My Story

Everyone who knows me knows three things: I love everything girly, my favorite color is pink, and I can always be found watching one K-drama or another when I have free time. These aren't just hobbies — they're the little pieces that make up my happy world.

One evening, I was tidying my shelf and found a small pink box I'd completely forgotten about. Inside was an old notebook, its pages filled with my messy handwriting from years ago. I'd written all my simple wishes there — to be kind, to have good friends, to do something that makes me happy. I smiled because those were still exactly what I wanted.

That night, I watched a warm, sweet K-drama as usual. But this time, something felt different. I realized that my life wasn't waiting for some big, exciting story to start. It was the story. The quiet moments, the laughter with friends, the dreams I held close — they were all chapters being written right now. ✨

🎓 My Future

My future isn't just about me — it's all about the people around me. Growing up, I really wanted to be successful so I can help other people who need help — with food, a place to live, and their daily needs. I want to be someone who can share what I have with those who have less. 💝

Aside from that, my biggest dream is to become an attorney. I want to be everyone's voice — especially for those who feel like they don't have one. I want to stand for what is right, fight for justice, and inspire others to stand up and speak the truth too. I know law school will be hard, but I'm ready to work for it. Because when you truly care about something, the hard work is always worth it. ⚖️✨
